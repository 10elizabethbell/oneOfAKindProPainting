# Product: One Of A Kind Pro Painting & Small Repairs LLC website

## Platform
web

## Stack
Static single-file HTML/CSS/JS (`index.html`), no frameworks, no tracking, system font stack. Hosted on GitHub Pages.

## Users
- **Homeowners, business owners and landlords** with a room, a wall or an exterior that needs paint, or small repairs that have piled up. The flyer names the audience itself: "Homes, Businesses, Rentals & More". Inferred: they arrive from a Facebook post or a shared link, on a phone, inside Facebook's in-app browser.
- **The owner**, who sees the site first. It has to look like their own business done properly within one phone screen.

## Purpose
1. Win over the owner: a ready-to-hand-over website for a business with no web presence today (one promo flyer in a Facebook group, 2026-10-18).
2. Deliver the "complimentary online estimate" the flyer promises: an estimate request a visitor can fill in on the page, handed off to the business by text message.

## Positioning
A painting and small-repairs business whose flyer leads with care and finish: "Details make the difference", "Quality work, honest service, affordable solutions", "A fresh start looks good here", "One of a kind. Inside and out."
